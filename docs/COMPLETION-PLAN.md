## Verification and completion rules

`npm test`, format checks, dependency audit and Docker CI must pass for each integrated release. Matter-archive tests invoke the offline verifier on generated metadata, full and empty-matter archives; checksums are not a provenance, encryption or non-repudiation claim. A test suite proves its exercised behaviors. Public benchmark reuse can involve training contamination; a document-level split prevents local overlap but cannot establish that a model never saw the source. Neither extraction F1 nor exact quote checks establish legal reasoning accuracy. The completion ledger must distinguish implemented, locally tested, live verified and externally unverified work.
